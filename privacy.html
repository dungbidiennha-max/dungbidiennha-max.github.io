Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Tram Live ("the app") is an internal tool that runs on our own computer to manage live streams on YouTube channels owned by our team.

Data we access

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to create and manage live broadcasts, live stream keys and video thumbnails, and to read the channel name to confirm the correct channel.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens and stream keys are stored only on our own computer and are used only to run our scheduled live streams. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted from our computer.
